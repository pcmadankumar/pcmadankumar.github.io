What it does

Everything you need to listen instead of read

Reports, textbooks, novels, scanned handouts, web articles: Shruti turns them into something you can listen to while you walk, cook or rest your eyes. No subscription, no account, nothing uploaded.

Natural voices, built in

Four natural neural voices (US male and female, British) that sound like a person reading, plus every voice already installed in Windows.

Scanned pages & photos

Scanned PDFs and screenshots are read with built-in text recognition (OCR), in English and Tamil. No retyping.

PDF, Word, EPUB and more

Drop in PDF, .docx, .doc, EPUB, TXT or images, or paste a link to a web article or a block of text.

Picks up where you stopped

Every document remembers its place, down to the sentence, so a book resumes exactly where you left it, days later.

Follow along

The sentence being read is highlighted word by word. Click any sentence to jump there; slow down or speed up from 0.5× to 3×.

Export to MP3

Turn any document into an MP3 in a natural voice and listen on your phone, on a walk or in the car.

Made for long reads

Chapters for books, bookmarks with notes, find-in-document, a sleep timer, and your keyboard’s media keys.

Private and offline

Everything happens on your PC. Your documents are never uploaded, and reading works without the internet.

Light & dark, your way

Dark or light theme, adjustable font size and column width, and per-document voice and speed.

Your listening library

Everything you’re reading, in one place

Shruti keeps every document you add, with its progress, so you can switch between a book, a report and an article without losing your place in any of them.

  • Continue listening puts the last thing you heard one click away.
  • Photos and scans become text the moment you add them, with progress shown for long scanned PDFs.
  • Web articles without the clutter: paste a link and Shruti keeps the article, not the menus and adverts.
  • Mark finished, search by title, delete what you no longer need.

Questions

Frequently asked

What happens after the 15-day trial?

Shruti asks for a licence key. Buy once, paste the licence key you get straight after paying, and carry on: your library and reading positions are exactly where you left them. There is never a monthly fee.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once and the licence never expires. Every 1.x update is included.

Does it need the internet? Are my files uploaded?

Your documents never leave your PC: reading, the natural voices and text recognition all run on your computer, so they work offline. Shruti goes online only to start your trial, to check your licence now and then, and to fetch a web article when you paste its link.

Which files can it read?

PDF (including scanned PDFs), Word (.docx and .doc), EPUB e-books, plain text, and images such as PNG, JPG and WebP. You can also paste text or a link to a web article.

Which languages does it support?

The four built-in natural voices read English. Text recognition reads English and Tamil, and Shruti can read any language for which a voice is installed in Windows, using the System engine.

How is it different from online read-aloud services?

Shruti is a one-time purchase rather than a yearly subscription, and it works on your own PC, so private documents stay private and nothing stops working when you are offline.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit) PC with about 1 GB of free disk space. Installing needs no administrator rights. The trial needs the internet the first time it opens, and the full version when you enter your key. Mac and Linux are not supported yet.

How do I pay from India?

Checkout is handled securely by Razorpay. In India you can pay by UPI, debit or credit card, net banking or a wallet; from outside India, by international card. The ₹ price is shown automatically when you visit from India, and the price shown is the price you pay.
