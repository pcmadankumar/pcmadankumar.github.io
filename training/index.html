Signature Workshops

Each with an outline, a duration, and what your teachers walk away with. All adapt to your school's context.

CBSE to Cambridge: Teacher Induction
Teachers moving to Cambridge Lower Secondary, IGCSE or A Level
2 hours Online or in person Delivered 3 times, 50+ teachers each
  • From rote to application: what changes in the classroom
  • Assessment objectives and command words, with past-paper practice
  • Curriculum planning for a Cambridge unit
  • Live polling and comparison challenges, not passive slides

Teachers walk away with: a command-word glossary, a unit-planning template, and a self-paced induction series in Microsoft Teams to continue after the session.

Cambridge Exams Officer Bootcamp
New Exams Officers and the exam office team
1 day, or 3 × 2-hour modules Built on the Cambridge Handbook 2026 90-minute invigilator briefing add-on
  • Entries, fees and the exam-cycle calendar
  • Coursework moderation, secure storage and despatch
  • Exam-day conduct, malpractice, special consideration
  • Results, post-results services and appeals — 24 scenarios, 70-question self-check

Teams walk away with: a dated exam-cycle checklist for their centre and a scenario bank to rehearse before each series.

AI for Educators: Practical Classroom Tools
All teaching staff, any board
Half day (3 hours) Hands-on, bring a laptop 8+ cohorts of 20+ since 2024
  • What AI can and cannot do for a teacher today
  • Lesson planning and differentiation with AI
  • Assessment and feedback: faster without losing judgement
  • Academic integrity and a safe-use policy for students

Teachers walk away with: three tested prompts for their own subject and a department AI-use checklist.

Supporting Multilingual Learners (CLIL)
Subject teachers in English-medium classrooms
3.5 hours in person, or two twilight sessions Four hands-on activities
  • Learning Design theory and the CLIL framework
  • Scaffolding strategies for content and language together
  • A real classroom case study, worked in groups
  • Reflection handout for follow-up in department meetings

Teachers walk away with: a teaching-strategy toolkit they can apply in the next lesson.
