The Training Team

Professional development at The Indian Public School is a system, not an event.

As Professional Development Coordinator I am part of the TIPS training team that runs whole-school training for 400+ staff, three times every year — May, September and December — across all four campuses. The team's job is to help each of them reach 50 hours of training in the year — planned, delivered, tracked, and reported on for impact. A usual cycle trains 400+ staff; the September 2026 cycle reached 480+, and the larger two-day May 2026 cycle had 600+ participants. The sessions below are the ones I designed, delivered, or co-delivered within that system, plus the exam-office and external training that sits alongside it.

Cambridge Curriculum & Exam Office Training

CBSE to Cambridge: Teacher Induction
Lower Secondary, IGCSE & A Level teachers Delivered 3 times · 50+ teachers each 2026 Online, 2 hours
An interactive induction for teachers moving from CBSE to the Cambridge curriculum: the shift from rote to application-based pedagogy, assessment objectives and command words, and curriculum planning — run with live polling and comparison challenges rather than passive slides. Delivered three times, to 50+ teachers each time, for each new intake — and now being expanded into a self-paced induction series in Microsoft Teams.
Cambridge Curriculum Command Words Curriculum Planning Live Polling

Teachers left with: a command-word glossary, a unit-planning template, and the self-paced follow-up path. Book this workshop.

Cambridge Exams Officer Training
Exams Officers across TIPS campuses 2018 – Present · latest ahead of the November 2026 series 3 modules
Exams Officer training and handholding for the TIPS campuses as Exam Controller — the full exam-administration cycle from entries and fees through coursework moderation, secure storage and despatch, exam day, malpractice, results, and appeals. The current edition is a three-module suite built from the Cambridge Handbook 2026 and a 20-question scoping survey, with 24 scenario exercises and a 70-question self-assessment; it was used to onboard the Trichy campus's new Exams Officer and then adapted into a centre-agnostic edition for external schools.
Cambridge Handbook 2026 Exam Administration Scenario-Based Self-Assessment

Outcome: Exams Officers able to run a Cambridge series independently, each with a dated checklist for their centre.

Behind the numbers is the data work the team does every cycle: training-hours dashboards per campus, a six-pillar classification of about 1,000 external trainings, and an impact model that ranks sessions by confidence gain and satisfaction. The six-pillar professional development framework and the trainer benefit policy are co-authored.
