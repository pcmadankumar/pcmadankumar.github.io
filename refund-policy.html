Refund Policy

When you can get your money back, and how.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Try before you buy

Every product lets you try first. Drishti and Shruti have a 15-day free trial with every feature. Each teacher kit has a free Starter download. Please use these to check that a product suits you before you pay.

When you can get a refund

You can ask for a refund within 7 days of buying if:

  • the app will not run on your PC (Drishti or Shruti), and we cannot fix the problem together; or
  • a file is faulty (a teacher kit file will not open or is damaged), and I cannot fix it or send you a working copy.

Refunds are not given for a change of mind, for buying by mistake after using the free trial or Starter, or after 7 days.

If you were charged twice for the same order by mistake, I will refund the extra payment in full, whenever you tell me.

How to ask

Email pcmadankumar@gmail.com with:

  • the email address you used to buy;
  • the product name and the payment reference from your receipt;
  • what went wrong. For an app, include your Windows version and any error message.

I will reply within 3 working days. Often I can fix the problem quickly. If I cannot, I will approve the refund.

How the refund is paid

  • Refunds go back to your original payment method (card, UPI, net banking or wallet) through Razorpay.
  • The money normally reaches you within 5–7 working days after I approve the refund. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.
  • You get back the full amount you paid.

After a refund

  • A refunded app licence key stops working. The app goes back to the locked state after the trial.
  • For a refunded teacher kit, the download links stop working, and please delete the files you downloaded.

Cancellations

All products are delivered instantly, so an order cannot be cancelled once payment is complete. The refund rules above still apply.

Contact

Refund questions: pcmadankumar@gmail.com.
