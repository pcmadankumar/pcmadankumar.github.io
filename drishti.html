What it does

Everything in your Markdown, drawn properly

Notes from AI assistants, project plans, course outlines, READMEs: Markdown is everywhere, but most apps show it as raw text. Drishti shows it the way it was meant to look.

Flowcharts & diagrams

Mermaid flowcharts, sequence diagrams and more are drawn with your own colours, on a clean white card in both themes.

Maths formulas

Inline and display LaTeX maths, typeset sharply. Prices like $5 stay as plain text.

Callouts, footnotes, emoji

Note, Tip and Warning boxes, clickable footnotes and heading links, task lists, and :emoji: shortcodes.

Print-ready PDF

A4 pages with page numbers, readable diagrams split cleanly between boxes, and wide tables turned landscape.

Word export

Editable .docx with real headings, bordered tables whose headers repeat on every page, and diagrams as pictures.

Private and offline

Everything runs on your PC. Your documents are never uploaded, and Drishti works without the internet.

Dark & light themes

Easy on the eyes at night, crisp in daylight. Your choice, window size and recent files are remembered.

Raw view & search

Switch to the plain text in one keystroke and find any word, with every match highlighted.

Fits into Windows

Right-click any .md file and choose Open with Drishti, or type Drishti notes.md in a terminal.

From screen to paper

Exports that look like you laid them out by hand

Share a plan with your team, print it for a meeting, or hand it in. No fiddling with page breaks.

  • Long flowcharts split between boxes, never through them, with a “(continued)” note on the next page.
  • Wide tables get a landscape page of their own; the rest of the document stays portrait.
  • Page numbers, repeating table headers and headings that never get stranded at the bottom of a page.
  • The same care in Word, so you can keep editing in Microsoft Word.

Questions

Frequently asked

What happens after the 15-day trial?

Drishti asks for a licence key. Buy once, paste the key, and carry on. Nothing is lost, and there is never a monthly fee.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once and the licence never expires. Every 1.x update is included.

Can I edit files in Drishti?

Drishti is a viewer: it is built to read, present and export Markdown beautifully. Keep writing in the editor you like (Notepad, VS Code, Obsidian) and open the file in Drishti to read, print or share it.

Does it need the internet? Are my files uploaded?

No and no. Diagrams and maths are drawn by libraries built into the app, so everything works offline and your documents never leave your computer.

Which diagrams does it support?

Anything written in Mermaid, the diagram language GitHub and most AI assistants use: flowcharts, sequence diagrams, timelines, pie charts, Gantt charts, mind maps and more.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit) PC with about 600 MB of free disk space. Installing needs no administrator rights. Mac and Linux are not supported yet.

How do I pay from India?

Checkout is handled by Gumroad and accepts Indian and international debit and credit cards and PayPal. The ₹ price is shown automatically when you visit from India.
