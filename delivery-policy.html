Delivery Policy

How you receive what you buy. Everything is digital.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Digital products only

Everything sold on this site is digital. Nothing is shipped or posted, and there are no delivery charges.

  • Drishti, a Markdown viewer for Windows: one-time licence, ₹899 or $9.99.
  • Shruti, a read-aloud app for Windows: one-time licence, ₹1,499 or $19.
  • Teacher kits for Cambridge IGCSE and AS & A Level teachers (9239, 9618, 0478 and 0457): downloadable files, ₹999 or $15 each, one-time. Each kit also has a free Starter download.

How you receive your order

Drishti or Shruti licenceYour licence key appears on screen as soon as payment is complete. It is also emailed to the address you gave at checkout. The installer is a free download from the product page.
Teacher kit (Full kit)Your download links appear on screen as soon as payment is complete, and are emailed to you as well. The Full kit includes the free Starter files.
Free Starter or free trialEnter your email and the download link appears on screen.

How long it takes

Delivery is instant. The email normally arrives within a few minutes. Please check your spam or promotions folder if you do not see it.

If you have not received it

If nothing has arrived within 24 hours of payment, email pcmadankumar@gmail.com with the email address you used and your Razorpay payment reference. I will send your licence key or download links by email.

Download links

Please download and save your files soon after you buy. If a link stops working later, email me and I will send a new one.

Contact

Delivery questions: pcmadankumar@gmail.com.
