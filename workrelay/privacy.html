Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

What WorkRelay stores

  • Logins created by your organisation: a username, a display name, a role and a scrambled (hashed) password. Passwords cannot be read by anyone, including the app's administrator.
  • Jobs and their updates: titles, places, deadlines, status changes, notes, and who did what and when.
  • Photos and voice notes added to jobs. These are stored in your organisation's own Google Drive, not on WorkRelay's servers.
  • A notification token for each phone, used only to deliver job alerts.

Google Drive access

When an organisation connects its Google Drive, WorkRelay asks only for permission to create and manage the files it creates itself (the drive.file permission). It cannot see any other file in that Drive. The organisation can remove this access at any time from its Google Account settings.

Who can see your data

People in your organisation see only what their role allows: a worker sees their own jobs, a lead sees their team's jobs, and the head sees the whole organisation. One organisation can never see another organisation's data. The app's administrator does not take part in daily work and looks at an organisation's jobs only when asked to help.

What WorkRelay does not do

  • It does not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • It does not show advertising.
  • It does not read SMS, contacts, notifications from other apps, or your location.

Keeping and removing data

Jobs and records are kept so the organisation has a history of its work. Logins are switched off, not erased, when someone leaves. To ask for data to be removed, contact your organisation's head, or the administrator below.
